Why snag at all?

Brand new doesn't mean finished.

Dubai's fast delivery timelines mean finishing work is often rushed. Most defects are invisible on a quick walkthrough with the agent, and much harder to get fixed once you've moved in.

01

Your leverage is highest before you sign

Before handover acceptance, developers are motivated to close out snags. Afterwards, your request joins a maintenance queue.

02

The costly problems are hidden

Hollow tiles, poor waterproofing, weak AC and wiring faults don't show up on a walkthrough. They show up months later as cracks, damp patches and summer breakdowns.

03

A clear report gets things fixed

A professional snag list with photos, locations and severity gives the developer's team exactly what they need to act, with no back and forth.

What we check

A checklist built for Dubai homes.

Heat, humidity, dust and district cooling create their own kinds of defects. Our checklist focuses on what actually fails in UAE properties.

AC & ventilation

  • Cooling performance per room
  • Thermostat & fan coil operation
  • Condensate drainage & leaks
  • Kitchen & bathroom extract fans

Electrical

  • Every socket: polarity & earth
  • RCD / earth-leakage trip test
  • Distribution board labelling
  • Lights, switches, balcony points

Plumbing & water

  • Water pressure & hot water
  • Leaks under sinks & vanities
  • Drainage flow & floor traps
  • Water heater installation

Waterproofing & wet areas

  • Shower falls toward drain
  • Grout & silicone continuity
  • Hidden moisture behind tiles
  • Balcony drainage & thresholds

Floors & tiling

  • Hollow tiles (tap test)
  • Lippage, levels & gaps
  • Cracked or chipped tiles
  • Skirting & transitions

Walls, ceilings & paint

  • Cracks & visible patching
  • Levels & straightness
  • Paint finish & coverage
  • Ceiling stains & access panels

Windows, doors & glazing

  • Seals against dust & water
  • Sliding door & lock operation
  • Scratched or damaged glass
  • Door alignment & hardware

Kitchen & appliances

  • Hob, oven, hood & dishwasher
  • Cabinet alignment & hinges
  • Worktop finish & joints
  • Sink, mixer & waste connections

Safety & balconies

  • Smoke detectors & fire points
  • Balcony railing height & fixings
  • Gas points where installed
  • Child-safety hazards

FAQ

Questions buyers and tenants ask us.

What is property snagging?

Snagging is a detailed inspection of a property to find defects, incomplete work and items that don't meet the agreed standard. The result is a snag list the developer, landlord or contractor can use to fix everything before you accept the property.

When should I book a snagging inspection in Dubai?

Book as soon as your developer issues the handover or inspection notice, and before you sign the handover acceptance. Once you sign, you have less leverage to get items fixed quickly.

Do DEWA and AC need to be connected?

Yes. Electricity, water and cooling (including district cooling where applicable) should be active so the engineer can test sockets, water pressure, drainage, water heaters and AC performance.

How much does snagging cost in Dubai?

Our pricing is based on property type and size. Use the instant price calculator for an indicative quote; we confirm the final price before booking.

Do I need to be there during the inspection?

No. Many clients are overseas. We coordinate access with the developer or agent and send you the full photo report. You're welcome to join if you'd like to see the property.

Can tenants book an inspection?

Yes. A move-in inspection records the condition of a rental before you move in, so you have dated photo evidence when it's time to get your security deposit back. More on tenant inspections.
